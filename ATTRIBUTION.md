# Vocabulary data attribution

JLPT deck data is imported from [stephenmk/yomitan-jlpt-vocab](https://github.com/stephenmk/yomitan-jlpt-vocab), derived from Jonathan Waller's JLPT vocabulary lists and distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Dictionary sequence identifiers refer to [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project), property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG).

The modern JLPT does not publish an official vocabulary syllabus. Level labels in this application are community classifications and should not be presented as official JLPT requirements.
